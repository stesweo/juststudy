# JustStudy Privacy Policy

JustStudy is a personal desktop study app.

**Google Tasks:** If you sign in with Google, the app asks for permission to view and manage your Google Tasks. It uses this only to show, add, complete and delete your tasks inside the app.

**Data storage:** Your Google sign-in token is stored encrypted on your own computer. Your tasks are fetched directly from Google and are not copied to or stored on any other server. No data is collected, shared or sold, and the app has no analytics.

**Revoking access:** Use "Sign out" in the Tasks panel, or remove JustStudy at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
